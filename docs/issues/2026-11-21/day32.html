Spin-offs often take place under pressure from activist investors. Stock analysts delight in the simplicity of the cleaved companies, and hedge-fund traders in the complexity of the separation. Buying spun-out shares is a foolproof strategy, wrote one investor, Joel Greenblatt, in “You Can Be a Stock Market Genius”. The book is dedicated to his wife and their “three magnificent spin-offs”. In the early 2020s few things were considered more toxic than skinny models and coal mining. Victoria’s Secret was liberated from Bath & Body Works, a soap-seller, in 2021.
