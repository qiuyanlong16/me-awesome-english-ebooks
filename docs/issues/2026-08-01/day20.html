After leading the CHP for over two years, Mr Ozel was deposed by a court decree in late May. When Mr Ozel’s supporters said they would not abide by the verdict, police stormed the CHP’s headquarters. Critics saw Mr Erdogan’s fingerprints all over the ruling. Turkey’s strongman sees Mr Kilicdaroglu as an opponent he can tame.
